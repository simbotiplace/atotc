Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Open the app, browse the menu, and add items to your cart. When you're ready, tap Checkout, enter your payment details, and confirm your order. You'll receive a confirmation with your order number right away.

How do I pay?

We accept major credit and debit cards securely through Square. Your payment information is processed by Square and is never stored on our servers.

How do I track my order?

After placing your order you'll see a live tracking screen showing each stage — received, confirmed, being prepared, and ready for pickup. The screen updates automatically so you always know where your order is.

When will my order be ready?

We'll show you an estimated ready time on the tracking screen once your order is confirmed. You'll also see a live countdown as your food is being prepared.

Can I cancel or modify my order?

Once an order is placed and confirmed by the kitchen it can't be modified through the app. Please contact us immediately at aagombedza@gmail.com and we'll do our best to help.

I was charged but my order didn't go through. What should I do?

This is rare but if it happens please email us at aagombedza@gmail.com with your order details and we'll resolve it promptly, including issuing a refund if applicable.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location access is required to verify that you're in a Square-supported payment region. It is only used at checkout and is never shared with third parties or stored by us.

Why does the app ask for Bluetooth and microphone access?

These permissions support Square card reader payments — Bluetooth for wireless readers and microphone for magstripe readers. They are only activated during a payment session.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login screen tap Forgot password? and enter your email address. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

How do I delete my account?

To request account deletion, email us at aagombedza@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my account. We'll remove your account and associated data within 30 days.

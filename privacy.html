Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ava's Taste of the Caribbean ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application ("the App").

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Account information: email address and password (stored securely and never shared).
  • Order information: items ordered, order status, and order history.
  • Device location: collected at checkout to verify you are in a Square-supported payment region. Not stored after the session ends.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not store payment card details. All payment processing is handled directly by Square. Please review Square's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle payment data.
  • We do not collect Bluetooth or microphone data beyond what is necessary to initiate a payment session with a Square card reader.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders.
  • To provide real-time order status updates.
  • To maintain your order history for reordering convenience.
  • To verify payment eligibility via location at checkout.

How We Store Your Information

Your account and order data is stored securely using Supabase, a managed database platform. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see below).

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is shared only with the following service providers strictly to operate the App:

  • Square — payment processing.
  • Supabase — database and authentication.

Your Rights

  • Access: you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: you may request that inaccurate data be corrected.
  • Deletion: you may request that your account and all associated data be deleted. Email us at aagombedza@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my account. We will action your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

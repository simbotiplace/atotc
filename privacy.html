Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026  ·  Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ava's Taste of the Caribbean ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application ("the App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data to serve you advertisements. We do not share your data with third parties except the service providers listed below that are strictly necessary to operate the App.

1. Data We Collect and Why

The table below maps each category of data to the Apple App Store privacy label we have declared for the App.

Data type What we collect Why Linked to you?
Contact Info Email address Account creation, order confirmations, password resets Yes
Identifiers Internal user ID Linking your orders and favourites to your account Yes
Purchases / Order History Items ordered, quantities, order totals, timestamps Fulfilling orders, order history, reordering Yes
Location Precise device location (at checkout only) Verifying you are in a Square-supported payment region Yes — session only, not stored
Usage Data Menu items browsed, cart interactions App functionality and improving the ordering experience Yes

What we do not collect

  • Payment card details — all card data is entered directly into Square's secure payment flow and never passes through our servers.
  • Bluetooth data — Bluetooth access is used only to communicate with Square card readers during a payment. No Bluetooth data is recorded.
  • Audio or microphone data — microphone access is required by Square for magstripe card readers only. No audio is recorded or stored.
  • Precise location outside of checkout — location is requested once at the point of payment and is not stored after the session ends.

2. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Your data is used solely within the App to provide the service you requested. The App does not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any cross-app tracking technology.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process, fulfil, and track your orders in real time.
  • To maintain your order history and saved favourites.
  • To send transactional communications (order confirmations, password resets).
  • To verify payment eligibility by confirming your location at checkout.
  • To improve the App based on aggregated, anonymised usage patterns.

4. How We Store Your Information

Account and order data is stored on Supabase, a managed cloud database platform. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest. Supabase infrastructure is hosted on AWS (us-east-1). We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

Password hashes are managed by Supabase Auth using bcrypt. We never store plain-text passwords.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data only with the following processors, strictly to operate the App. Each is bound by their own privacy policy and data processing agreements.

Provider Purpose Data shared
Square Payment processing Payment card details (entered directly into Square's UI — not seen by us)
Supabase Database, authentication, real-time updates Email address, user ID, order data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third party.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that inaccurate data be corrected.
  • Deletion — request that your account and all associated data be permanently deleted. Email aagombedza@gmail.com with the subject line Delete my account. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.
  • Portability — request a machine-readable export of your data.
  • Opt-out — you may stop using the App at any time. Deleting the App does not delete your account — use the deletion request above to remove your data.

If you are a resident of California, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and to opt out of any sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information).

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and order data for as long as your account remains active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by applicable law (e.g. financial records required for tax purposes).

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or provided personal information, please contact us immediately at aagombedza@gmail.com and we will delete the information promptly.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including TLS encryption in transit, encryption at rest, and role-based access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your account credentials confidential.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For material changes we will make reasonable efforts to notify you (for example, via an in-app notice). Continued use of the App after a change takes effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
